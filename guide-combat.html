COMBAT GUIDE

Combat Fundamentals

Learn how to approach neutral, attacks, defence and punish opportunities without relying on mindless aggression.

COMBAT UBG GUIDE
01
THE BASICS

Understand neutral

Neutral is the part of a fight where neither player has a clear advantage.

This is where you decide whether to approach, defend, bait an attack or wait for your opponent to make the first mistake.

A common beginner mistake is treating every moment of neutral as an invitation to attack. Instead, use this time to gather information.

KEY IDEA The player who understands the opponent's habits usually has an advantage before the next exchange even begins.
02
OFFENCE

Attacking effectively

01

Do not attack automatically

An attack should have a purpose. You might be trying to pressure your opponent, punish an action or force them into a defensive option.

02

Avoid predictable patterns

If you repeatedly use the same attack or approach in the same situation, your opponent can begin preparing for it.

03

Know when to stop

Getting a hit does not mean you should immediately continue attacking. Sometimes the safest option is to reset the fight.

04

Make your opponent react

Strong offence is not simply about throwing more attacks. Pressure can force your opponent into predictable defensive decisions.

03
DEFENCE

Defence is more than blocking

Good defence is about controlling risk. Blocking, movement and spacing can all help you survive an opponent's pressure.

BLOCK

Protect yourself

Use your guard when you expect an attack and avoid giving your opponent free openings.

MOVE

Change position

Movement can help you create distance or reposition yourself after an exchange.

READ

Predict behaviour

Pay attention to repeated habits and recognise what your opponent prefers to do.

05
MINDSET

Adapt to your opponent

AGGRESSIVE

They rush you

Avoid matching their aggression automatically. Look for predictable approaches and defensive mistakes.

DEFENSIVE

They wait

Be patient. Test their reactions rather than repeatedly throwing attacks into their defence.

PREDICTABLE

They repeat actions

Once you recognise a pattern, change your response and punish the habit.

UNPREDICTABLE

They constantly change

Do not overcommit to a prediction. Focus on fundamentals and react to what actually happens.

06
PRACTICE

Improve one thing at a time

01
Focus on defence

Spend a few matches concentrating on recognising attacks instead of winning.

02
Study your mistakes

When you get hit, think about what caused the opening instead of immediately blaming your opponent.

03
Identify habits

Pay attention to what opponents repeatedly do after attacking, blocking or getting hit.

04
Build consistency

Once a skill becomes reliable, move on to another part of your gameplay.
