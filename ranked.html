OVERVIEW

Competitive Boxing

Ranked is a competitive gamemode unlocked at 40 knockdowns. Players are assigned ranks through RT points and are matched against other players with similar scores.

Winning increases your RT, while losing decreases it. Players gain more RT for defeating players with higher scores and lose more RT when defeated by players with lower scores.

LEAGUES 5 Total leagues
SYSTEM

How Ranked Works

Ranked uses an RT-based rating system to determine a player's position within the ranked ladder.

Your RT changes after ranked matches depending on the relative scores of the players involved.

WIN + RT Winning increases your rating.
→
RT RANK Your rating determines your rank.
→
LOSS − RT Losing decreases your rating.
!
Rating difference matters

Players gain more RT for defeating opponents with higher scores and lose more RT when defeated by players with lower scores.

Placement Matches

PLAY 05 PLACEMENT MATCHES

Find Your Starting Rank

When entering Ranked for the first time, players are presented with an interface telling them to complete five placement matches.

These matches are used to evaluate the player's general skill level.

MORE WINS Higher placement
MORE LOSSES Lower placement
