ABOUT

Gloves

Gloves are the main cosmetic items in UBG. They can be obtained through crates in the shop, Ranked rewards, trading and limited-time events.

Gloves are purely cosmetic and have no impact on gameplay statistics.

Gloves come in four main rarities: Uncommon, Rare, Mythic and Legendary. Gloves obtained from crates can also have a rare Unusual effect.
