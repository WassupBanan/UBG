BEGINNER GUIDE

Getting Started

Everything you need to know before stepping into the ring. Learn the controls, understand the basic combat loop and start building good habits.

BEGINNER UBG GUIDE
01
START HERE

Welcome to the ring

Untitled Boxing Game is built around reading your opponent, managing your stamina and choosing the right moment to attack.

You do not need to memorise complicated techniques immediately. Start by learning the basic controls, understanding when to attack and, most importantly, learning when not to attack.

BEGINNER TIP Good players do not simply attack more. They make better decisions with each attack.
02
FUNDAMENTALS

Basic controls

M1

Light Punch

Your basic attack. Use it to pressure your opponent, start combinations and punish openings.

M2

Heavy Punch

A stronger attack that can be useful for dealing damage and punishing predictable actions.

F

Block

Protect yourself from incoming attacks. Blocking at the right time can stop your opponent's pressure.

SPACE

Dash

Quickly reposition yourself, create distance or help avoid an incoming attack.

COMMON MISTAKES

What beginners should avoid

01

Constantly attacking

Throwing attacks without thinking can leave you predictable and vulnerable.

02

Ignoring your opponent

Do not focus only on your own attacks. Watch what your opponent is trying to do.

03

Panic dodging

Dodging randomly can make your movement predictable and waste valuable opportunities.

04

Chasing every opening

Not every opening needs to be punished. Sometimes resetting is the safer choice.

05
TRAINING

A simple practice routine

01

Learn your style

Spend time understanding your chosen style's attacks, strengths and weaknesses.

02

Practice blocking

Focus on recognising attacks instead of trying to win every exchange.

03

Practice movement

Work on dashing and repositioning without wasting movement unnecessarily.

04

Fight real opponents

Once you understand the basics, play matches and pay attention to what repeatedly catches you off guard.
