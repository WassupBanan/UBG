About UBG Mechanics

Untitled Boxing Game is built around timing, movement, stamina management, defensive play and reading your opponent. This page collects the major mechanics used during combat.

Wiki note: Some mechanics can change between updates. Numerical values, frame data and exact inputs should be treated as version-sensitive unless verified.
