Mechanics

A guide to the combat systems and gameplay mechanics that make Untitled Boxing Game work.

⚙

Untitled Boxing Game is built around timing, movement, stamina management and reading your opponent. Understanding the game's mechanics is just as important as choosing the right fighting style.

This section documents the core systems used during combat, from basic punches and blocking to advanced mechanics such as perfect dodging and feinting.
