ABOUT

Knockdown Animations

Knockdown animations are cosmetic effects that play during a knockdown or knockout sequence. They do not replace a fighting style and are separate from emotes, gloves and other collection items.

Some animations are associated with references to anime, games, memes or other pop-culture scenes. Availability and shop listings can change as UBG is updated.

COLLECTION NOTEAnimation ≠ gameplay effect

These cosmetics are presented for their visual animation and theme rather than combat statistics.
